About the game

Your spells cast themselves. You just have to survive.

Runebound Depths is a fast, one-thumb dungeon roguelike. Guide a little pink wizard through randomly built dungeon floors while your spells fire on their own at every monster in reach. Keep moving, dodge, and grow your build until the room is clear.

BUILD A NEW COMBO EVERY RUN

  • Pick a new spell or upgrade after every room
  • Fire, frost, lightning, venom and arcane spells that level up and combine
  • Rare legendary spells and boss spells

DIVE DEEPER

  • 50 floors across crypts, woods, caverns, forges, frozen deeps and swamps
  • Rooms of every shape and size, survival rooms, dark rooms and trap rooms
  • Champions that stalk each floor and a boss every third floor

LOOT AND GROW

  • Hats, robes, staffs, amulets, rings and boots with random stats
  • Lucky chests, a gold shop and shrines inside the dungeon
  • Soul shards and permanent upgrades between runs, plus Legacy levels that make every new run stronger

MADE FOR PHONES

  • Drag anywhere to move, that's it
  • Short runs you can play in a few minutes
  • Plays offline

The game is free to play and contains ads.
