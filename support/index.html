Common questions

How do I play?

  • Drag anywhere to move, that's it
  • Your spells cast themselves at every monster in reach
  • Pick a new spell or upgrade after every room

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. Runebound Depths has no accounts or cloud saves, so progress can't be moved to another phone, and uninstalling the app deletes it.

Why are there ads?

Runebound Depths is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: occasional full-screen ads between runs and floors, and optional videos you can choose to watch for a reward such as a revive. They keep the game free.

How do I change my ad choices?

If you are in a region where consent is required, the game asks before showing personalised ads. You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier at any time. On Android, open Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by phone). On iPhone or iPad, open Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Does it work offline?

Yes, Runebound Depths plays offline. Ads only load when you are connected.
